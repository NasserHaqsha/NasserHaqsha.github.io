    Deraa: AI cybersecurity compliance engine

    AIEP project, team of 4, 2026

    Reads a company's cybersecurity policies in Arabic or English and checks them against the NCA's NCNICC-1:2025 controls. Every verdict comes with a verbatim quote from the policy as proof.

    1. FindHybrid retrieval: BM25 plus BGE-M3 embeddings in FAISS, with a reranker.
    2. JudgeA local LLM rules on the control and returns structured JSON.
    3. VerifyAn evidence guard rejects any quote that is not in the policy word for word.
    4. ScoreCompliance scores are computed in code, not by the LLM.
    Each control ends ascompliantpartialnon-compliant
    65main NCA controls checked
    47sub-controls
    22subdomains routed by a fine-tuned AraBERT, benchmarked against TF-IDF
    0cloud calls: it runs fully offline on local models
